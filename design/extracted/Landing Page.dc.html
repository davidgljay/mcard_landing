This is for communities that already exist

A club that meets, a network that gathers, a group with a channel and regulars who know each other. If you've already built the trust, cards give it somewhere to go.

Your community can create shared resources anywhere on the internet

  • Give every member a card. Anything your community makes can check it.
  • No accounts, no passwords, no membership database to maintain.
  • Anyone in the community can set something up, not just you. The calendar doesn't have to be your job.
  • It works anywhere on the internet, not inside one app.

Your members can prove their affiliation to others

  • A card proves your members belong, to anyone, anywhere — and reveals nothing else about them.
  • Members can show affiliation when they need to be taken seriously: reaching out to an institution, joining an adjacent community, asking for resources.
  • Anyone can check a card and see who vouched for it and why — following the chain back to something they already trust.
  • Your members choose what to show and when. The cards someone holds aren't connected to each other.
We can't see who holds your community's cards or what they do with them. That isn't a promise we make — it's a thing the system can't do.

How It Works

Every card carries a chain of verification. When someone shows you a card, you can see who issued it, why, and why the issuer is who they say they are.

No one has to move to a new platform
  • Your group chat, calendar, or channel stays exactly where it is.
  • Cards layer on top of what your community already uses.
You can update or revoke cards whenever you like
  • A card reflects current standing, not past standing.
  • Revocation is authoritative — it can't be hidden from a verifier who checks.
Your community's data will remain private and secure
  • No single operator can see who holds a card or what they do with it.
  • The data belongs to your community, not to us.

Care about community? We'd love your help.

The membership card protocol has been an open source labor of love for Relationality Lab and the community surrounding it. We have outlined the protocol and deployed the infrastructure to make it a reality. But it still needs to undergo rigorous third party auditing and we still need communities to test it with. We are holding off on building the front-end infrastructure that people will use to manage their cards until we have a set of community partners to build with.
